Decisions not in the spec:

- **State shape.** `GameState` gained `lives`, `graceStart :: Maybe Double` (Round time at which the running Grace began; active iff `roundTime < graceStart + gracePeriod`), `paused`, `windowFocused` and `roundResult :: Maybe RoundResult`. `Input` gained `Blur`, `Focus` and `Click`. `Over` keeps `Phase`'s existing constructor.
- **`RoundResult`** is `{resPoints, resTotalPoints, resWpm, resAccuracy, resKills, resLongestStreak, resStreak}`; `resTotalPoints` is the Total Points after banking. At Over the state's own `totalPoints` also becomes carried-in plus Round Points, so the HUD's Total and the Summary agree with the saved value.
- **Banking guard.** `roundEnded old new` (Running -> Over only) is the guard; the wiring derives `RoundEnded` from `current gameState` and `updated gameState`, so a re-emitted Over never banks. `bankRound :: RoundResult -> Save -> Save` adds `resPoints` only (Streak and Bonus are saved per Kill or Typo). The app root merges it with the progress edit using `mergeWith (.)`.
- **Re-Hit timing.** A re-Hit starts its Grace at the scheduled end of the last one, not at the Tick that noticed, so a parked Enemy costs a Life every Grace Period without drift. Contact is `distance <= contactDistance + 1e-6` (parked Enemies sit there up to rounding).
- **Blur mechanism.** In the pure step: a paused Tick only advances `lastTime` (dt 0, so no jump on resume). Resume needs `Focus` first (`windowFocused`), then any `Key`, `Tab` or `Click`, swallowed. The key shim reports `Blur`, `Focus` and `Click` (window `mousedown`, capture) and any other key as `Other`, and has no state of its own. Escape and Enter are also swallowed-or-not by the same rule: Escape always leaves; Enter is `Key '\n'` to the step (resumes when paused, otherwise ignored) and Play again only while Over. Enter with a button or link focused is reported as `Other`, so Enter on "Back to start" is not also Play again.
