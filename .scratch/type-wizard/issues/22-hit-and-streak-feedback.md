Decisions not in the spec (also in the README, "Hit and Streak feedback"):

- **Time base and records.** Two more start-time records on the Tick clock (`lastTime`): `hitAt :: Maybe Double` and `meterAnim :: Maybe MeterAnim`. The Round-time `graceStart` and `groupCompletedAt` cannot serve because `roundTime` is a clamped clock that stands still while paused; `groupCompletedAt` stays as ticket 18 made it. The step only creates records (Hit, Kill, Typo) and expires them (`expire`, every Tick in every Phase); the builder evaluates everything at `Frame.time`.
- **Hit time.** `hitAt` is the Tick that noticed the Hit, so a re-Hit at the scheduled Grace end is up to one frame late; the Hit that ends the Round sets it too (the flash finishes behind the Summary).
- **Decay shape.** The spec's "decays over 0.3 s" is a linear decay from 0.30 to 0.
- **Blink phase and Round end.** Full opacity first, then dim, alternating every 0.1 s from the Hit, only while the Grace Period is active in the state. Round end clears Grace, so the blink stops there and never shows in `Over`; the flash (a 0.3 s decay) finishes behind the Summary. While the window is blurred, Grace is frozen (it runs on Round time), so the blink and the flash go on at the builder's clock.
- **Overlay.** One screen-fixed red `Square` at the end of `sceneOverlay` (over the HUD), centred, `worldHeight * aspect + 2` by `worldHeight + 2`, present only while its alpha is above 0, so it always covers the view and costs nothing at rest.
- **Meter rest equals ticket 18.** With no record the meter is the static view of the Streak, node for node as before (the old Scene tests pass unchanged).
- **Back-to-back rule.** Every event starts from the view displayed at `lastTime` (evaluated from the old record), so the display is continuous: Kill after Kill grows from the shown fill; a Kill or Typo during a completion's flash or emptying starts from the shown fill (the white drops); a Typo fades the label from its shown scale and alpha, keeping its colour and text; a Kill during the pop finishes the pop at its own speed (the record then lasts as long as the pop needs). Only one record exists, so there is no growth.
- **Completion colour.** White during the flash, then the Streak's `shotColour` (the Streak after the Kill) while emptying; the Typo keeps the pre-Typo colour while it empties.
- **Label scale** is the `Text` transform's scale, so it grows about the label's left anchor, the way the text sits.
- **Constants** (blink period, dim alpha, flash alpha and time, the meter and label times, the pop scale) are in `Game.Layout`, not settings, as the ticket's settings list has none.
- **Hook** (`?hook`) gained `hitAt` and `feedback` (`playerAlpha`, `flashAlpha`, `meterFill`, `meterWhite`, `labelBonus`, `labelScale`, `labelAlpha`, `meterAnim`), all evaluated at `lastTime`.
- **Browser check method.** The 0.1 to 0.3 s animations cannot be hit with screenshots, so the check wraps `WebGL2RenderingContext.prototype.drawArraysInstanced` and, right after each draw, reads the hook and pixels with `readPixels` (one record per frame): the canvas pixels are the evidence, the hook gives the model. The canvas can trail the hook by one or two frames, so pixel comparisons are within a time window; the Word of a parked Enemy can cover the Player's centre, so the Player is sampled at its corners (a first version sampling the centre failed twice in four runs for that reason). Screenshots (`game22-*.png`, read with the image viewer) show before and after.
