Decisions not in the spec (also in the README, "Camera"):

- **Record shape and time base.** `CameraAnim { caStart, caFrom, caTarget, caTau }` in `GameState.camera`, in the Tick-clock base of ticket 20 (`lastTime`, the rAF timestamp); the step makes a record only when the Focused Enemy changes and the target differs, starting from `cameraCentreAt lastTime` of the old record, so the retarget is continuous with the last frame drawn. A Kill or Tab record starts up to one frame early (sub-frame skew, as for Kill records); 0 before the first Tick.
- **Held-target rule.** The target is computed at Focus time from the Enemy's position then and held for the whole Word (Focused and Locked). Reason: the Enemy only moves toward the Player, both lie inside the held visible rectangle, which is convex, so the Enemy stays visible; chasing would make the view drift while typing.
- **Round end.** Over drops Focus, so the Camera eases back behind the Summary. The builder's clock keeps running, but Ticks in Over do not move `lastTime`, so the hook's `camera.centre` is frozen there (the record and pixels show the return).
- **`Start`** resets the Camera to rest on the Player without easing.
- **Per-axis smallest shift** is applied once the Enemy is outside the default view on either axis, so an axis in the inset zone is also shifted; an Enemy fully inside the view (inset zone included) causes no pan.
- **Spec contradiction, resolved (margin vs inset).** The 2-unit inset and the 3-unit Player margin conflict for far Enemies: at world height 20 and 16:9 the vertical clamp is 7 units, so an Enemy straight above on the spawn ring (radius about 21) is not brought on screen by the pan (it comes closer as it walks). The Player margin wins, as the ticket and the spec's "the Player stays at least 3 units inside every screen edge" say. With a margin of at least the half-view the Camera cannot move on that axis (settings allow up to 8 against a half-height of 4 at world height 8).
- **Setting name.** The Settings field is `playerMargin` (the ticket calls it `cameraPlayerMargin`); `cameraTimeConstant` and `cameraInset` are as named.
- **Background square** follows the Camera centre (it used to follow the Player), so the panned view stays covered.
- **Hook** gained `camera` (`start`, `from`, `target`, `tau`, `centre` at `lastTime`).
- **Spec deviation: none**; section 14 items 1, 5 and 9 hold.
